Dreamcatcher

Loop your playing and shape it with delay, pitch shifts, grains, and reverb.

Dreamcatcher records as you play. Tap the foot switch to loop a short part of your playing. Add delay, pitch shifts or reverb to the loop or your live sound.

The dry signal is your guitar without effects. The wet signal has effects. The delay can break sound into grains, which are short slices that can shift pitch, reverse or repeat. A slow control wave, the LFO (low-frequency oscillator), sets the pace of modulation and chance events.

Chase Bliss MOOD MKII and Old Blood Noise Endeavors Parting inspired the effect. Dreamcatcher has no affiliation with either company and contains none of their code.

Source and releases on GitHub

Controls

ControlAction
Turn PARASelect a setting; the screen shows its section and value
Turn the volume knobEdit the selected setting after a small move; gold text shows that the knob has taken control
Tap the foot switchCatch a loop, or release it and record new playing
Hold the foot switchAdd sound to a playing loop (DUB); while listening, freeze the wet signal (FRZ)
Press PARASave the sound to the active preset, except when choosing a different preset; see below
Hold PARA for 3 sToggle the effect; bypass keeps the dry signal unchanged and retains the caught loop

Overdub (DUB) adds your playing to the loop until you release the foot switch. Freeze (FRZ) holds the wet sound while you hold the switch.

When you select a setting, it keeps its value until you move the volume knob a small amount. The knob then sets the value from its own position, which can cause a jump. It edits the selected setting; select VOLUME to set output level.

The LEDs glow violet when the effect is on and dim red when it is off. The header dot pulses at the LFO rate.

A good start

  1. Play a chord and tap the foot switch to catch it as a loop.
  2. Set ROUTE to IN+LOOP so the effects process both your guitar and the loop.
  3. Raise SMEAR and set CHANCE a little above its middle value.
  4. Lower CLOCK, then play over the loop.

In the tables, noon means the middle of a setting’s range. The knob must take control before its position sets the value.

Settings

Tempo sync needs Dreamcatcher 1.4 or later. Other tables describe the controls shared by these builds.

MAIN

SettingWhat it does
PRESETSelect one of 8 saved sounds; press PARA to load the highlighted slot
MIXBlend dry and wet; the middle gives both at full level
CLOCKSet the engine’s sample rate, from 1.4 to 44 kHz in musical steps
SYNCChoose FREE timing or SYNC to the BPM tempo
BPMSet tempo from 40 to 240 beats per minute
VOLUMESet output level

On PRESET, pressing PARA loads a highlighted saved slot or saves into an empty one. With the active slot highlighted, it saves the current sound. Away from PRESET, pressing PARA also saves to the active slot. A preset holds all settings, including VOLUME.

P3 means preset 3 is active. P3* means its sound has changed since the last save. 3 EMPTY marks an empty slot. SAVED P3 confirms a save.

A lower CLOCK gives longer loops and delays, with a rougher sound. Changing CLOCK during a loop shifts its pitch in musical steps. In SYNC mode, TIME, RATE, LENGTH and GRAIN use note lengths, and the LFO follows the beat. A dot means a dotted note, T means a triplet, and BAR means one bar.

WET

SettingWhat it does
TIMESet delay time: 4 ms to 1.16 s at 44 kHz, or up to 37 s at lower CLOCK values
SMEARBlend distinct echoes into dense reverb through diffusion and feedback
TONEDarken each repeat; OPEN removes that darkening
CHANCESet how often sound enters or feeds back through the delay
GLITCHSet delay-clock speed or let the LFO choose it at random

With SYNC, TIME spans 1/32 note to 1 bar.

CHANCE has three regions:

  • Below noon, it sets the chance that input enters the delay on each LFO cycle.
  • At noon, the delay takes all input (ALWAYS).
  • Above noon, input keeps entering and feedback becomes more likely (FB).

GLITCH also changes behavior across its range:

  • Below noon, choose HALF, NORMAL or DOUBLE delay-clock speed.
  • Above noon, each LFO cycle has a growing chance to choose a new clock speed and crush amount (RND).

GRAIN

SettingWhat it does
GRAINSet slice length: 6 to 186 ms at 44 kHz; with SYNC, 1/64 to 1/16 note
PITCHSet the chance that a grain shifts to an interval in SCALE
SCALEChoose OCT UP, OCT DN, OCTAVES, 5TH+OCT or HARMONY
REVERSESet the chance that a grain plays in reverse
STUTTERSet the chance that a grain repeats the one before it

Feeding pitch-shifted repeats back into the delay builds a shimmer.

MOD

SettingWhat it does
RATESet LFO speed: 0.05 to 12 Hz; with SYNC, 1/32 note to 4 bars per cycle
DEPTHSet the amount of modulation
SHAPEChoose SINE, SQUARE, RAMP, SAW, RND SIN, RND SQR or ENV
MODApply TREMOLO (volume changes) or VIBRATO (pitch changes) to the wet signal

RATE also sets how often CHANCE makes a choice. With ENV, your playing level opens the delay and CHANCE becomes SENSE, the sensitivity control.

COLOR

SettingWhat it does
DISSOLVEBelow noon, reduce sample rate (LOFI); at noon, CLEAN; above noon, reverse at full, ½, ¼ or ⅛ speed
FILTERBelow noon, low-pass from 150 Hz to 13 kHz; at noon, OPEN; above noon, high-pass from 20 Hz to 2.5 kHz

A low-pass filter cuts highs. A high-pass filter cuts lows.

LOOP

SettingWhat it does
MODEChoose TAPE or STRETCH
LENGTHSet loop length in TAPE mode or slice length in STRETCH mode
SPEED / DRIFTSet tape speed and direction, or how fast the slices move
LOOP LVSet the loop’s level and how much it feeds the effects
FADESet how much old sound remains on each overdub pass: REPLACE to 100 %
ROUTEProcess IN (guitar), IN+LOOP or LOOP in the wet channel

In TAPE mode, LENGTH spans 1/16 of memory to all of it: up to 1.47 seconds at 44 kHz or 47 seconds at 1.4 kHz. With SYNC, it spans 1/8 note to 4 bars. SPEED spans REV 4X to 4X.

In STRETCH mode, LENGTH sets slice size. DRIFT moves through slices in reverse or forward, with FROZEN at noon to hold the position.

REVERB

SettingWhat it does
DECAYSet tail length from 0.3 to 27 s; HOLD sustains the tail and blocks new input
MIXBlend from no reverb, through effect plus reverb at the middle, to reverb alone
SIZEChange room size from a room to a large hall
PRE-DLYWait 0 to 200 ms before reverb starts
MODAdd slow pitch drift to the tail
SHIMMERFeed an octave-up copy of the tail back into the reverb

Reverb MIX changes the wet path. Your dry guitar stays dry.

Presets in Launchpad

Use Launchpad’s Presets page to edit sounds as you play and save them. You can name, import, export and move the 8 preset slots. Use Write to pedal to save slot edits. Backups save the set of presets to a file.
